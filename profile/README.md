## What is SOWA Privacy?

SOWA Privacy is the only browser-level AI privacy proxy that works **entirely independently of any AI platform**. It detects and anonymizes personal data in real time — **before** it reaches any AI system — with zero cloud infrastructure and zero changes to your existing AI workflows.

> *The owl sees all. SOWA ensures none of it leaves you.*

## How It Works

```
Your Prompt  →  SOWA detects & anonymizes PII  →  Anonymized prompt sent to AI
                     (all local, on your device)
```

1. **Install** the SOWA Privacy extension from the Chrome Web Store
2. **Activate** and configure your privacy presets
3. **Browse** and use AI tools as normal — SOWA handles the rest

No cloud. No configuration. No compromise.
